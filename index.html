Mortars, cements and plastic refractories

Mortars and cements bond refractory bricks at high temperature and improve lining integrity. Plastic refractories are mouldable for patching and quick repairs.

Mortars and cements

  • Refractory mortarFor laying and jointing refractory bricks in furnaces and kilns.
  • Fire clay mortarHeat-resistant mortar for use with fire clay bricks.
  • High alumina mortarStrong bonding and resistance to elevated temperatures.
  • Air setting mortarReady to use; hardens on its own when exposed to air.
  • Fire cementFor jointing and repairs in high-temperature areas.
  • A.R. cementAcid-resistant cement for laying A.R. bricks.

Plastic refractories

  • Fire clay plastic refractoryMouldable material for patching, repairs and complex linings.
  • High alumina plastic refractoryBetter thermal shock resistance and mechanical strength.
  • Furnace repair plasticReady to use for quick repairs and restoring damaged linings.

About Bimal Ceramics

Bimal Ceramics Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated on 13 August 1986. For nearly four decades we have supplied refractory products and technical support to industries across India, from our registered office and works in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh.

We manufacture and supply refractory bricks, castables, ramming and gunning mixes, mortars, plastic refractories, bottom pouring sets, custom shapes, ceramic products and boiler bed material.
